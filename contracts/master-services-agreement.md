# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by
Ironbark Fabrication Co. (the "Supplier") and Halcyon Field Services (the "Buyer").

## 1. Scope

The Supplier shall provide the goods and field services described in the
purchase orders issued under this Agreement. Each purchase order forms a
separate contract incorporating these terms.

## 2. Term

The Agreement begins on 2026-10-01 and continues for twenty-four (24)
months unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 9.

## 3. Payment

Invoices are payable net thirty (30) days from the invoice date. Partial
payment does not discharge any remaining balance. The Buyer may withhold
payment for disputed items provided written notice is given within ten
(10) business days of receipt.

## 4. Delivery and Acceptance

Delivery dates are stated on each purchase order. Acceptance occurs after
inspection at the Buyer's site or written confirmation of completion for
services.

## 5. Warranty

Goods are warranted against defects in materials and workmanship for
twelve (12) months from acceptance. Services are warranted to be
performed in a professional and workmanlike manner.

## 6. Change Control

Any change to scope must be documented in a signed change order. Verbal
instructions are not binding unless confirmed in writing.

## 7. Compliance

The Supplier shall comply with all applicable environmental, safety and
labour regulations and shall maintain adequate insurance coverage.

## 8. Liability

Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at the
total fees paid or payable during the preceding twelve (12) months, save
for fraud, wilful misconduct and death or personal injury.

## 9. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach on thirty (30) days' written
notice if the breach remains uncured, or immediately upon insolvency of
the other party.

## 10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon.

Executed for Ironbark Fabrication Co. on 2026-09-28 and for Halcyon Field Services on
2026-09-30.
